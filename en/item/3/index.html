What We've Done

The largest student CS conference in East Asia

We inspired SITCON x HK and the Chongqing Student Open Source Conference, and we support student clubs and tech groups across regions. Each year, over half of our attendees are first-timers; alumni work across the tech industry in Taiwan, the U.S., Japan, and the Netherlands—and regularly return to give back.

Meetups across North, Central, and South Taiwan

We co-host meetups with local communities, offering students channels to exchange knowledge. We often collaborate with schools like National Taiwan University and Tamkang University, bringing together outstanding talents from everywhere.

SITCON Hour of Code

Since 2014, in partnership with the Ministry of Education, we've regularly hosted Hour of Code events across Taiwan, sparking curiosity for computing among hundreds of children aged 4–16.

SITCON Hackathon

We held HackGen in 2014 and 2015, and in 2024 we scaled up to the SITCON Hackathon. Participants learn to ideate, plan, and ultimately build—so society and industry discover high-potential student teams.

SITCON Summer Camp

Since 2014, every July we run a summer camp to help students get started with CS and ignite passion for open source. Students serve as both campers and volunteers. Beyond the core curriculum, we host open-source talks, a community expo, and the Vision Café—so learning is paired with rich opportunities to connect and exchange ideas.

How students apply for support

Enrolled students with financial need can apply for travel and lodging support through the following steps.

  1. Students submit an application with proof of enrollment and financial need.
  2. SITCON reviews eligibility and documents, then sends invitation codes to approved applicants.
  3. Students complete registration with the code to gain admission.

The official application rules use first come, first reviewed. Places depend on funding; applications close when funds are exhausted.

How is this different from the Far-Away Ticket?

Far-Away Ticket

  • Reserved tickets for students in high-need areas, outlying islands and overseas
  • Admission opportunity
  • No travel or lodging subsidy

Open Dream Program

  • Enrollment and financial need are eligibility conditions
  • Admission after review
  • May apply for travel and lodging the night before the event
