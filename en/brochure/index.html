What can SITCON offer?

Talent Recruitment

Over a thousand CS students in one day. Booths, exclusive sessions and complimentary tickets put you face to face with future engineers.

Brand Exposure

From venue backdrops and the R0 video loop to social media and email pushes, keep your brand in the most active student community.

Product Promotion

Co-branded tickets, welcome-bag inserts and booth demos let students try your product on site.

Education Support

Open Dream Program: help students in need attend the conference, with exposure reserved exclusively for program sponsors.

SITCON 2027 EDUCATION & CONTACT

Open Dream Program

Every year some students cannot attend because of travel and lodging costs. The Open Dream Program is funded by sponsors and provides a ticket plus round-trip transportation (bus, train or HSR) and lodging the night before the event, so students with financial need can join SITCON. Estimated at about NT$3,000 per student.
